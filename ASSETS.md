### What the generator keeps

- Body, eyes, teeth, and eyelashes. Joint cubes, tongue, genitals, and the helper skirt are not exported.
- Male: muscle 0.78, weight 0.22 (lean, not bulky). Female: muscle 0.66, weight 0.26. Both use the young targets only.
- Ancestry blend on the macro targets: caucasian 0.42, african 0.30, asian 0.28.
- Up to four bone influences per vertex, renormalized. The full default skeleton is kept so fingers follow the wrist.
- Short hair is a scalp shell. The female also has a small ponytail skinned to the head.
- Outfit shells share the body weights: boardshorts, bikini top and bottom, rashguard, and a springsuit/onesie. Colors and patterns come from `src/data/catalog.json` at runtime. The menu defaults are red boardshorts (male) and the teal bikini (female). The fullsuit is available for both.

### Compression

The two glTF binaries are a few megabytes combined and are embedded in the JavaScript bundle. They are not Draco or meshopt compressed, and they have no KTX2 or WebP textures, because skin and cloth are materials rather than images and because a WASM decoder would have to be fetched at runtime. That fetch breaks the single-file `file://` build. Quantizing or decoding inside the page would also add a second large blob for little gain at this mesh size.

Regenerate with:

```bash
node scripts/build-surfers.mjs
```
