# Assets

Code in this repository is MIT, Copyright © 2026 Ed Ostrowski. See [LICENSE](LICENSE).

The surfers are the only third-party art. Everything else — water, foam, spray, sky, the island (`src/island.js`: terrain, rock, white water and palms, all generated from a seed and shaded in a shader), boats, surfboards, and the clothes the surfers wear — is procedural and original.

## Surfers

Generated by `scripts/build-surfers.mjs` into `src/assets/surfers.data.js`: two glTF binaries (male and female meshes, skeleton, skin weights, UVs), base64, and the textures as base64 data URIs, so the single-file build stays self-contained. Raw downloads are cached in `.cache/` (gitignored) and are not committed.

### What the generator does with them

- **UVs.** `scripts/lib/mh.mjs` reads `vt` and the `v/vt` face corners; a vertex on a texture seam is split into two (one per UV), so the glTF has `TEXCOORD_0` for every mesh that has a texture.
- **Skin.** The four skin maps are 2048 px. Each is divided by its mean colour over torso, arms and legs and stored at 60% grey (JPEG q86): a detail map (tone variation, freckling, redness, stubble) with no base tone. The game multiplies it by the catalog skin colour, so every one of the six tones keeps reading as its swatch (`scripts/skin-calibrate.mjs`). Deep and brown use the dark-skinned maps, bronze to light use the light-skinned maps (chosen from renders: the dark map on bronze gave a green cast around the mouth). A small procedural pore bump (generated in `src/surfer.js`, no image file) adds fine grain.
- **Baked into the skin (made here, not downloaded).** MakeHuman's skin and hair `.mhmat` files name no normal map, bump map or scalp mask, so `scripts/lib/skinbake.mjs` makes two things: per-vertex cavity shading (hollows such as eye sockets, nostrils and mouth corners are darker; written as `COLOR_0`), and a scalp mask (each skin texel is ray-cast at the fitted hair mesh and darkened under opaque hair, so the hairline and strand gaps show a dark scalp on every skin tone).
- **Hair colour (made here).** The two CC0 hair maps are one fixed colour each (`short04` is near black, `ponytail01` brown with orange tips) and a material tint cannot turn black into blond, so `scripts/lib/haircolour.mjs` re-colours the colour map: the strand detail (luminance) is stretched between a shadow colour and a light colour, and low-frequency value noise stretched along the strands adds sun-bleached streaks. Male: dark brown to sun-bleached dark blond. Female: brown with golden streaks. The hair alpha is untouched.
- **Eyes, brows, lashes, hair.** Fitted to the shaped body with MakeHuman's vertex-fitting data (`.mhclo`, documented in `scripts/lib/mh.mjs`), skinned to the same bones (hair: the head bone). Hair colour (JPEG 1024) and hair alpha (greyscale PNG 1024) are separate, alpha-tested; brows and lashes keep only their alpha (greyscale PNG 512).
- **Clothes are not downloaded.** The pack holds no swimwear (its 20 clothes are shoes, a hat, and casual, work, elegant and sport suits). Trunks (with a drawstring), bikini top and bottom, rashguard, fullsuit and springsuit are cut from the body surface by `scripts/lib/garments.mjs` along smooth iso-lines, pushed out from the body, and share the body's skin weights. They are original work, MIT with the code.
- **Teeth** are the base mesh's own helper teeth (flat colour).
- The base mesh's helper geometry for joints, tongue, genitals, skirt and tights is not exported.

Treat the derived meshes and textures as CC0 as well.

### Sizes (see `_reports/m2-look.md` for the measured numbers)

Textures total about 4.4 MB as data URIs, the two glTF binaries about 4.2 MB before base64. Not Draco or meshopt compressed, and no KTX2 or WebP, because a WASM decoder would have to be fetched at runtime and that breaks the single-file `file://` build.

Regenerate with:

```bash
node scripts/build-surfers.mjs
```

Node 20 or newer, network on the first run only (later runs read `.cache/`). Dev dependencies `pngjs` (MIT) and `jpeg-js` (BSD-3-Clause) decode and encode the images; they are not shipped in the game.

## Prints on outfits and boards (made here, no files)

Stripes, chevron, sunburst, palm, tiger and panel prints are drawn in code at runtime (`src/printart.js`, 2D canvas, no image files and no third-party artwork) into single-channel masks: 2048 px per metre tiles for garments (the largest 256 x 512 px, the panel tile 1024 x 64), 900 px per metre plan-view masks for boards (1584 x 472 px for the shortboard). The colours come from the item, not the mask. The garments' print UVs are computed at load from the MakeHuman shells (`src/printuv.js`); the body atlas UVs they were cut with are not used for prints.
